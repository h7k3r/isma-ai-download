# i.S.M.A Ai

Your projects, as a command centre on your phone — with an assistant that can run on your own AI or a friend's machine.

## Download (Android)

**[Download isma-ai.apk](https://github.com/h7k3r/isma-ai-download/releases/latest/download/isma-ai.apk)**

Open the file on your phone. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or files app — allow it, then install.

## Email confirmed

If a confirmation link brought you here: you're done. Open the i.S.M.A Ai app and sign in with the email and password you chose.

## Using a friend's rigs

If a friend gave you a rig key:

1. Install [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/download) and accept the share invite your friend sent.
2. In the app, open the assistant's settings and enter the rig address, model and key they gave you.

Their computer has to be on for this to work. When it isn't, the app still runs its simple built-in commands.
